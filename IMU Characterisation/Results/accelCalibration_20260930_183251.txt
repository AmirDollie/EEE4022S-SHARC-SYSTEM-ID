NOTES
 - gain error = reading / true - 1 (positive reads high). Offsets do not affect the transmissibility.
 - "calibration scale unc." is the uncertainty of the calibration itself (leave-one-out half-range or
   hold-noise floor), not a 95% bound and not a measured post-calibration match: H1b makes that claim.
 - a sphere fit fixes axis lengths, not directions: misalignment to the board frame is not estimated.
 - dof = holds - 6 parameters. With dof = 1 the residual RMS is weak evidence and leave-one-out is
   unavailable (needs >= 8 holds), so the uncertainty is the hold-noise floor only. Add tilted holds
   (about 45 deg, three roll angles) to test the model; the full model needs >= 12 holds.
 - the two-point z estimate assumes the up/down holds were aligned with z; a tilt of theta biases
   it by about (1 - cos theta), 0.06% at 2 deg. The fit does not need aligned holds.
